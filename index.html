How to play
  • Drag your finger from a colored dot toward its matching dot.
  • The path can't cross another color's pipe — go around it.
  • Drag back over your own pipe to shorten it.
  • Grab a pipe anywhere along its length to redraw from that point.
  • Connect every color to win. Cover the whole board for a perfect score.
  • Every puzzle is randomly generated and always has a solution.
